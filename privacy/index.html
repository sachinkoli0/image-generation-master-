Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Image Generation Master is a prompt-engineering skill distributed as project files. The skill itself does not operate an independent account system, analytics service, or user database, and does not independently collect or transmit personal information.

When used within ChatGPT or another assistant platform, prompts and uploaded materials are handled according to that platform's privacy terms and settings. If prompts are copied into a third-party image-generation service, that service's privacy policy and data practices apply.

This website may be hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as request logs under its own policies.

For project privacy questions, contact the maintainer through GitHub Issues.
