About the project

Creative thinking, translated into prompts.

A practical visual library for creators who want more control over image-generation results.

What this project is

Image Generation Master is a prompt-engineering resource focused on turning visual references and creative concepts into detailed, structured image prompts. The studies break a visual down into useful parts such as subject, composition, wardrobe, environment, lighting, camera language, typography, post-processing and negative constraints.

How to use the library

Browse the gallery, open a study, inspect its structured JSON, and copy the prompt as a starting point. Adapt the details to your own subject, reference, generator and aspect ratio rather than treating a prompt as a one-size-fits-all recipe.
